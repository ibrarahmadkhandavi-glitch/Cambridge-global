FAQ

Is posting a listing free?+
Yes. Direct listings are always free and commission-free. Broker-assisted listings carry a 1% commission only when the sale closes.
How does the Pakistan ↔ Japan corridor work?+
Sellers list auction-grade imports from Japan; buyers in Pakistan browse and contact them directly to arrange shipping and clearance.
Can I compare multiple cars?+
Yes — tick "Compare" on up to 3 listings and use the compare bar at the bottom of the screen to view them side by side.
Does CarBridge Global handle payments?+
No. Buyers and sellers deal hand-to-hand. CarBridge Global only connects the two sides.
